Privacy policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

In short: 10K collects no personal data. Everything you enter in the app stays on your phone; nothing is sent to the publisher or to anyone else.

The 10K app

The app works entirely offline: it doesn't even request permission to access the Internet.

  • What is stored, and where: player names, aliases and their colors, scores, game history, language and settings. This information is stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage.
  • What doesn't exist: no account, no ads, no analytics, no trackers, no sharing with third parties.
  • Deleting your data: uninstall the app, or go to Android Settings → Apps → 10K → Storage → Clear data. Nothing is kept anywhere else.

This website

  • This website and the app's installation file are hosted by GitHub (GitHub Pages and GitHub Releases). Like any host, GitHub may technically log visitors' IP addresses, notably for security — see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.
  • The site itself sets no cookies, doesn't measure audience and loads nothing from another service (the font is hosted on the site).
  • The home page has a link to support the project on Ko-fi. It opens Ko-fi's website: what happens there is covered by the Ko-fi privacy policy, not this one.

Children

Since the app collects no data, it collects none about children either.

Your rights

The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) gives you, among others, the right to access, correct and erase your data. As the publisher holds no data about you, there is nothing to consult or delete on their side: your data is on your phone, and you keep full control of it. You can also contact the French data protection authority, the CNIL, if you believe your rights are not respected.

Contact and changes

A question? Write through the project's “Issues” page on GitHub. If this policy changes (for example if a future version of the app needed the Internet), the new version will be published on this page with its update date.
